09 / The origin

The reason
before the name.

Guion began with a north-star question: could a new general-purpose language earn a recommendation over Python for real work, through the whole experience of writing, checking, running, delivering and maintaining a program?

  1. 22 Oct 2026

    The question was written down.

    Under the working name Veritas, the first design put usability, one coherent toolchain, honest comparison and executable evidence at the center.

    docs/north-star-design.md v0.2, “approved build direction.” Veritas, then.

  2. 25 Oct 2026

    It became Guion.

    The implementation and its tools were renamed. The earlier results stay on file under their original name.

    Commit 6a4fab1, “rename Veritas product surfaces to Guion.” Veritas-era checkpoints and results are preserved as history; selected legacy manifests and files remain readable.

  3. 27 Oct 2026

    The latest checkpoint.

    The results on this page come from it, including the ones where Guion is still behind. It is a checkpoint, not a finish line.

    Build at commit 6b5e5aba, artifact format 44, macOS ARM64. Summary in docs/status.md.
